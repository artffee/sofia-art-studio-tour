The Artist (and your guide)

Ivaylo, the painter behind IVOSART

Ivaylo is the abstract expressionist painter behind IVOSART and the founder of Art Gallery Captain Nemo.

This is not a guide reading from a script. You walk with the artist himself: through his gallery, into the studio where the work is made, and on to the art bazaar on Shishman Street. Every visit is led personally, in English or Bulgarian.

Good questions

How long is the adventure?

The Studio Walk takes 2.5 hours. Captain Nemo Night takes 3 hours.

Will it be a crowd?

Never. Up to 8 guests on the Studio Walk and up to 6 on Captain Nemo Night.

Do I need to speak Bulgarian?

Not at all. Tours run in English and Bulgarian, and every visit is led personally by Ivaylo.

What's in the price?

Studio access, all art materials, wine and your own finished artwork. Captain Nemo Night adds the candlelit atmosphere, a longer painting session and a signed original small print to keep.

Where exactly do we go?

Three stops: Art Gallery Captain Nemo, the A02x Studio and Punto Art Bazaar on Shishman Street, ending with your own painting.

Will I really leave with a painting?

Yes. You paint your own small abstract piece and carry it home. On Captain Nemo Night you also keep a signed original small print.

Can I bring my whole team?

Yes. Private groups, couples and corporate team events are available on request. Write to us for private dates and group bookings.
